Latest entries

Quoted from the gem's CHANGELOG.md, which follows Keep a Changelog and Semantic Versioning. Long entries are cut: the full text is in the file.

0.47.0 2026-11-07

Added

  • Jobs: The jobs a request or a job puts on a queue are recorded when they are enqueued (EnqueuedJobsCollector) and listed by the toolbar route as enqueued_jobs until they start and get a profile of their own, matched by job id.
  • Live events: GET /_profiler/api/stream is a server-sent events stream that announces, as it happens, a change to any of the profiles a client follows or to what is under them (AJAX calls, jobs, jobs of jobs), including a job saved by another process. config.live_events (true in development only) turns it on and config.live_events_max_streams (2) caps the threads it holds; over the cap, with too few Puma threads, or when off, it answers 503 with Retry-After.

Changed

  • Jobs: A job's save is now announced to every profile above it, up to five levels, so the request at the top sees the end of a job of a job.

0.46.1 2026-10-28

Fixed

  • Release: A release that did not complete now blocks the branch pipelines until it is repaired, and the release job no longer stops silently when several versions are waiting.

0.46.0 2026-10-28

Added

  • Tests: RSpec and Minitest are hooked automatically when track_tests is on and the framework is loaded: no RSpecSupport.install or MinitestSupport.install in the app.
  • Runner: rails runner executions are profiled, as console profiles with source: "runner": the code or script path, the return value and the failure with its exception, saved before the process exits. track_runner is on in development, runner_skip lists what to leave out.
